    How to read this page
    The words used below, in plain terms    
      
    
        
    Prior authorization
    Approval a doctor must get from the patient's health plan before giving a drug, so the plan will pay for it.
    
        
    Kanini Context
    The approved rules, each checked against its official document, which an AI (or plain code) asks instead of reading the documents again.
    
        
    The answers
    No prior authorization needed · Prior authorization required · Coverage criteria not met (no approval needed, but the patient does not meet the policy's medical conditions) · Missing patient information · Send to staff.
    
        
    Case
    One made-up patient and one drug order, for example an Ohio patient on epoetin alfa (an anemia drug) under Original Medicare. There are six.
    
        
    Run · Right
    A run is one attempt at a case; each case was answered 5 times by each method, so 30 means 6 cases × 5 runs. Right means the same answer the approved rules give.
    
        
    Facts given
    The patient's values already pulled out and labelled: hemoglobin 9.4 (a blood count), eGFR 52 (a kidney function value), the diagnosis codes, not on dialysis.
    
        
    Patient chart
    The patient's record as a clinic keeps it: lab results with their dates and units, the diagnosis list, and doctors' notes. To use it, the AI has to find the values itself, such as the latest hemoglobin, in the right units.
    
        
    Tokens
    How much text the AI reads and writes to answer one case. AI services charge by the token, so fewer tokens means cheaper and faster.
    
        
    Search (RAG)
    The common way to give an AI documents: find the few passages that best match the question and send only those.
    
        
    LCD
    A Local Coverage Determination: a Medicare contractor's written policy for its region, such as LCD L39237 for Tennessee and L34356 for Ohio.
    
        
    Billing code (HCPCS)
    The code an order uses for a drug, such as J0885 for epoetin alfa. The rules are looked up by this code.
    
        
    The pack
    The approved rules Kanini Context holds: the plans, regions and drugs that have been curated so far.
    
        
    Guardrail
    A check in code, not in the AI, that makes the recorded answer match the rules' answer whatever the AI wrote.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    AI tokens per question
    Average text the AI read (input tokens) to answer one case.
